Why we exist

Fernhill started with one simple belief: a few green things can change how a room, a street and a day feel.

Our aim is to make healthy plants easy to find, easy to afford and easy to look after, so that anyone can bring a little green into their life, whether they have a sunny garden or a single windowsill.

Healthy, well-grown plants

Every plant is checked, watered and settled in before it reaches the shelf.

Care advice with every plant

Light, water and pet safety are listed for each plant, and we are happy to talk you through it in person.

Greener neighborhoods

We give back by supporting community planting days and school garden projects.
